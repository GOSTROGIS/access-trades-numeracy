How this calculator works

TI-30Xa: algebraic entry

Key the first number, the operation, the second number, then = for the answer: shows

Functions of one number

Key the number first, then the function: shows in DEG.

Mixed numbers

Press a b/c between the parts of a mixed number: shows

Angle units

DRG steps through DEG, RAD and GRAD. The calculator starts in DEG, and the screen shows the unit in use.

Rectangular and polar coordinates

  • Rectangular to polar: x, x⇄y, y, then R▸P shows r. x⇄y, R▸P and P▸R are 2nd functions. shows
  • Then x⇄y shows θ: shows
  • Polar to rectangular: r, x⇄y, θ, then P▸R shows x, and x⇄y shows y. shows

Memories

Three memories, 1 to 3. STO 1 stores the display in memory 1, RCL 1 brings it back, and SUM 1 adds the display to memory 1.

Digits

The TI-30Xa works with 13 digits inside.

Clearing

ON/C clears. The worked examples start with ON/C ON/C and DEG on the screen.
