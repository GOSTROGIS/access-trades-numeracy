How this calculator works

Sharp EL-531XT: D.A.L., keys in the order the line is written

Key the calculation in the order it is written, then press =. The calculator works the line out with its priority levels (XT manual, Priority Levels in Calculation, TH guide p. 11): shows

Functions of one number

sin, log, √ and the other functions go before the number, the way they are written: shows in DEG. x², x³ and x⁻¹ go after the number (TH guide pp. 13, 22-23).

Second functions

2ndF, then a key, gives the function printed above that key (TH guide p. 3).

Mixed numbers

Press a b/c between the parts of a mixed number (XT Examples [8], TH guide p. 19): shows

Angle units

DRG steps through DEG, RAD and GRAD (TH guide p. 6). The screen shows the unit in use.

Rectangular and polar coordinates

  • Rectangular to polar: key x, then 2ndF and the comma key, then y, then 2ndF →rθ. The screen shows r (TH guide p. 25). shows
  • Then 2ndF ←,→ shows θ: shows
  • Polar to rectangular: key r, then 2ndF and the comma key, then θ, then 2ndF →xy. The screen shows x, and 2ndF ←,→ then shows y (TH guide p. 25). shows
  • The two results are also kept in memories X and Y (XT manual, Coordinate Conversion).

Memories

STO A stores the display in memory A and RCL A brings it back. The letters A, B and C are the ALPHA labels on yˣ, √ and x² (TH guide pp. 3, 20).

Display

Up to 10 digits, in plain form from 0.000000001 to 9999999999 (TH guide p. 4). Inside, it keeps up to 14 digits (XT manual, Modify Function). Groups of three digits are marked off with a raised comma, as in (XT manual, Calculation Examples).

Clearing

ON/C clears (TH guide p. 7). The worked examples start with ON/C ON/C and DEG on the screen.
